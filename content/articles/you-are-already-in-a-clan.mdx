If you have been here before, you know about the Fog, and you know it was not your fault. That was the first thing. Here is the next: the way out is not the same for everyone. Which way in is yours. That is what we are here to find out.

Sit with these for a moment. Don't answer them. Just notice which one puts a hand on your chest.

What is true?

What truly matters?

What will you do?

What needs healing?

One of those landed harder than the others. You felt it before you could explain it. That is not a mood. It is the shape of how you have been moving through the world since before you had words for it.

You came in tilted. Everyone does. Long before you chose a single thing about your life, you were already angled one way, toward understanding, or toward loyalty, or toward action, or toward feeling. One of them tends to lead. It led when you were seven and it leads now, in the small choices you don't notice yourself making. This is not a test result. It is not a type you get assigned. It is a plain fact about how you have actually lived. The name is not a box we put you in. It is the place you start from.

Here are four people. You know all of them. One of them you know from the inside.

There is the one who always needed to know why. Rules were never enough; the reason under the rule was the only thing that would hold still long enough to be trusted. This one felt the lie in the room before anyone said a word, watched the adults smile and knew the smile was covering something, had no way to prove it and no one to tell. Air moves like that, ahead of the announcement. The Whisperers carry a particular kind of grief, the grief of the person who saw it coming. Things that did not have to go the way they went, went that way anyway, and this one watched, and remembers each one.

There is the one who stayed. When everyone else was chasing the next bright thing, this one built. A garden, a marriage, a craft, a house that other people could stand inside of. The Rooted Ones endure, which is a plain-looking word for something most people cannot do. And they lose things, the way anyone who stays long enough loses things, and they do not make a show of it. You will not see this one weeping in the road. You will see them the next morning, up early, tending what is still alive. The coffee goes cold while they work. The grief is real. It just goes underground, where the roots are.

There is the one who moved first. Thought came after, if it came at all. This one has burned bridges that other people are still upset about, and lit fires that warmed a hundred people who never learned whose fire it was. The Flameborn know both halves of that and do not pretend to know only the flattering one. To act before you are certain is to be wrong sometimes, publicly, in ways that cost you. It is also the only way anything actually starts. This one has the scars to prove the first part and the light to prove the second.

And there is the one who felt your pain before you mentioned it. Walked into a room and knew who in it was quietly falling apart. The Deep Kin carry other people's weather, and they learned to do it so young that they mistook it for weather of their own. This is the one who knew where everyone else's edges were and could not, for the life of them, find their own. Still can't, some days. Still learning that a boundary is not a betrayal.

You know which one is closest to the glass.

Something has been using your nature against you. Whatever that nature is, the thing working against it is not some general darkness that comes for everyone. It fits your shape like a key. That is what makes it hard to see.

For the Whisperers it is Deceit, and the lie is not the one you tell other people. It is the one you told yourself, the story that was so elegant, so internally consistent, that you never checked whether it was true. It explained everything. That was the tell.

For the Rooted Ones it is Attachment. There was a thing you would not put down, and holding it cost you three other things, and you told yourself the three were the price of loyalty. They were the price of not letting go.

For the Flameborn it is Stagnation, which sounds like the opposite of your nature until you see it clearly. The speed was the hiding place. You kept moving because if you ever stopped, the thing you have been outrunning would finally catch up and sit down across from you.

For the Deep Kin it is Resentment. You built a story about someone who wronged you, and you tended it so long that it got more real than the person ever was. You are still married to the story. The person left years ago.

You know which one you have been fighting. You have known for a while.

Before you decide any of this, something is already watching. The first card every player receives is the White Antlered Wolf. Part spirit, part teacher, it walks between worlds and it does not belong to any one clan. The antlers are perception; the wolf is instinct. It runs between all four paths, and it arrives before you have chosen yours, which is the point. You do not find the wolf. The wolf finds you, and it finds you through your nature, the one you were born tilted toward.

And when it does, you don't sign anything. You pick up the shard by doing something real, out in the world, with your actual hands. Intent, then action, then the evidence that it happened. That is the whole mechanism. That is the door.

We closed the last one by asking whether you had seen the wolf. Here is the truer version. The wolf has already seen you, and it saw you through the lens of what you carry, the question that put its hand on your chest a few minutes ago. Air sees one thing. Earth another. Fire and Water their own. The wolf reads you in the language you were born speaking.

You already know which lens that is. The first question told you.
